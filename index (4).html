Our Story

Why Ganitshaala Exists

I still remember being the student who dreaded the word "maths" — until one teacher showed me it was just a language for patterns, not a pile of rules to memorise. That shift is the reason Ganitshaala exists: not to drill formulas, but to help students see the pattern behind the problem.

After years of working as an engineer, I started Ganitshaala with a handful of students in a small room. Today we run two centres across Chandigarh — but every batch is still small enough that no student is "just a roll number."

Chetan Founder & Lead Educator, Ganitshaala

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to things parents and students usually ask before enrolling.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes — alongside in-centre batches we offer online sessions for students who can't travel to Chandigarh, with recordings available for CUET batches.

What is the batch size?

Batches are kept small so every student gets individual attention and doubts get addressed in real time — not lost in a crowd.

Is study material provided?

Yes — notes, practice worksheets and previous years' papers are included in every course at no extra cost.

How do I book a free demo class?

Click "Book a Free Demo Class" or message us on WhatsApp with your name, class and preferred centre — we'll confirm a slot within a few hours.

What are the fees?

Fees vary by course and batch. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share the full fee structure for your specific course.

Do you track student progress?

Yes — weekly tests and regular progress updates keep students and parents informed every step of the way.
